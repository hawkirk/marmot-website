The first release of Marmot. After two years of writing the app while writing a novel inside it, Marmot is on the Mac App Store.

### Editor

- Distraction-free editor built on `NSTextView` — no input lag at chapter or novel length.
- Optional focus mode dims everything but the sentence under the cursor.
- Smart typography handles em-dashes, smart quotes, and ellipses as you type.
- Per-document word count, session count, and daily goal.

### Reader

- Beautiful typeset preview of any chapter or the whole manuscript.
- Serif body with generous leading and balanced measure.
- Search and navigate without leaving the page view.

### Compile

- One-click PDF export in **industry-standard manuscript format** for novel submissions.
- Choose Courier or Times New Roman; both are accepted by editors.
- Export an individual chapter or the entire book.

### Files

- Manuscripts stored as plain Markdown files on your Mac, in a folder you choose.
- Compatible with Git, iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Time Machine, or any backup tool.
- Optional `marmot.json` manifest captures order and metadata; never required.

### System requirements

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
- Apple Silicon and Intel Macs both supported.
